**A simple python program, that allows you to roll dice, flip coins, 
generate shapes, and also calculate interest if you're into that.**

To try it out follow the instructions at: rng.mhisham11.hackclub.app

**FEATURES:**
-Coin flipper:
Flip lots of coins, and keep track of the total results

-Dice roller:
Roll as many dice as you wish, and get your results as a sum and also text art.

-Interest Calculator
Calculate simple or compound interest, by entering the amount invested, for how long, and at what rates.

-Shape Generator
This one is my favourite. 
It lets you type in the name of a shape to draw, then it draws it using python turtle graphics.
